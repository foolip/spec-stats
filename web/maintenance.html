Maintenance load

Who does the work of keeping the WHATWG Living Standards going?

Maintaining a standard is mostly reviewing other people's changes, merging them and answering issues. This page counts that work on GitHub: reviews of someone else's pull request, merges, and comments on someone else's issue or pull request. Each person is attributed to the organization named on their public GitHub profile. Bots and self-reviews are left out.
